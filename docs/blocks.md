# The node list

Every block Spindle registers, which part of the world introduced it, and
why it exists. **Rule (2026-09-09): at most one new material per biome on
the surface**, and any addition gets a heads-up before it lands. The
placeholder set the shape was built with is grandfathered; everything from
the temperate woodlands on is listed against its biome.

Textures are generated by `tools/make_textures.py`; the colours live there.
**The palette is Lord of the Rings** (rule, 2026-09-10): muted, earthy, a
little grey in everything — moss greens, umber soils, hillside stone,
dark green-blue water. Nothing saturated.
**Every texture is one flat colour** (rule, 2026-09-10): a picture never
carries variation. What breaks up a surface is the renderer's — the
large-scale `tint`, and per-cell value jitter once the engine has it (asks,
item 5). A textured material is a decision the designer makes by name.
